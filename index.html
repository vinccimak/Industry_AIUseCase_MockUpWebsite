UPS × ORION

AI-Powered Dynamic Route Optimisation

ORION uses operational data and optimisation algorithms to create efficient delivery routes, then adapts routes as traffic conditions and delivery commitments change.

01

Intelligent Route Planning

  • Analyses delivery stops, collection commitments, package information, and route constraints.
  • Creates an efficient initial route before drivers begin their daily deliveries.
02

Dynamic Optimisation

  • Recalculates routes when traffic conditions, road incidents, or delivery commitments change.
  • Provides drivers with updated route guidance while they are already on the road.
03

Operational Impact

  • ORION has been associated with approximately 100 million fewer miles travelled annually.
  • UPS estimates annual fuel savings of approximately 10 million gallons through route optimisation.

01

Full-Vehicle HD Visibility

  • Supports up to four auxiliary 1080p HD cameras, or up to eight with a Dual AI Multicam setup.
